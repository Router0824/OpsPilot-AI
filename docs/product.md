# Product brief

OpsPilot AI is an AI-native operations workspace for product teams, AI teams, and small organizations. It turns fragmented documents and meetings into knowledge, decisions, actions, risks, workflows, and durable organizational memory.

The core demo follows an internal AI product launch: review workspace health, approve Meeting Intelligence outputs, inspect the blocked task and detected risk, ask for a weekly operations report, and review workflow analytics.

All bundled content is synthetic and contains no proprietary organizational information.
